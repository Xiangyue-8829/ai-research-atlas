AI Research Landscape & Research Framework

A structured guide to 29 core AI research fields, with links to their subtopics, research status, open problems and interdisciplinary connections.

This is an editorial research map, not an exhaustive systematic review or a ranking of research maturity. Open questions are research hypotheses or published gaps, not guaranteed opportunities.

A. Learning & Foundations

Mathematical foundations of learning from finite data, optimization, and causal identification.

A1 · Learning & Foundations

Statistical Learning Theory

PAC learning, generalization bounds, and sample complexity have established theoretical foundations; understanding modern deep networks remains an active research area.

Representative topics

PAC / PAC-Bayes · VC Theory · Rademacher Complexity · Algorithmic Stability · Generalization Bounds

Open research questions

  • How can we derive tight generalization bounds for overparameterized networks?
  • What guarantees are possible for out-of-distribution and interactive data?

A2 · Learning & Foundations

Optimization for Machine Learning

SGD, Adam, and related optimizers are widely used; the dynamics and efficiency of large-scale nonconvex training remain active topics.

Representative topics

Convex Optimization · Nonconvex Optimization · SGD / Adam · Stochastic Approximation · Second-order Optimization

Open research questions

  • What theory can explain optimization in large nonconvex models?
  • How can training stability, computation, and optimality be balanced?

A3 · Learning & Foundations

Learning Paradigms

Supervised and unsupervised learning have mature toolkits; self-supervised, active, and weak supervision continue to expand.

Representative topics

Supervised Learning · Unsupervised Learning · Self-supervised Learning · Semi-supervised Learning · Active Learning

Open research questions

  • How can learning depend less on human annotations?
  • How should noise and bias in weak supervision be addressed?

A4 · Learning & Foundations

Representation, Transfer & Continual Learning

Large-scale pretraining has improved transfer, but continual adaptation, representation stability, and identifiability remain challenging.

Representative topics

Representation Learning · Contrastive Learning · Transfer Learning · Domain Adaptation · Domain Generalization

Open research questions

A5 · Learning & Foundations

Probabilistic & Bayesian ML

Probabilistic modeling and MCMC have mature foundations; scalable inference for complex latent, non-Gaussian, and neural models remains active.

Representative topics

Gaussian Processes · Latent Variable Models · Bayesian Neural Networks · Probabilistic Graphical Models · GMRF / SPDE

Open research questions

  • How can high-dimensional posterior approximation balance speed and accuracy?
  • Is uncertainty reliable under model misspecification?

A6 · Learning & Foundations

Causal Learning & Inference

Potential-outcome and graphical identification theories are established; high-dimensional confounding, interference, spatial-temporal causality, and causal representations remain active.

Representative topics

Causal Discovery · Potential Outcomes · Structural Causal Models · Causal DAGs · Counterfactual Learning

Open research questions

  • What can be concluded when observational data do not identify a causal effect?
  • How do interference and distribution shift affect causal estimands?

Autoregressive Generation · Variational Autoencoders · GANs · Diffusion Models · Score-based Generative Models

Open research questions
